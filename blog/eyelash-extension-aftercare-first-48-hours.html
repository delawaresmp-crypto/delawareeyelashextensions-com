Eyelash extension aftercare: the first 48 hours

What to do and what to avoid right after your appointment so your new set bonds well.

The adhesive keeps curing for a while after you leave. What you do in the first two days has a big effect on how long your set lasts.

The first 24 hours

  • Keep your lashes dry. No crying at movies, no swimming, no face washing over the eyes.
  • Avoid steam. That means saunas, steam rooms and very hot showers.
  • Skip workouts that make you sweat a lot.
  • Do not touch, rub or pull at your lashes.

Hours 24 to 48

  • You can wash your face, but keep water pressure off your eyes.
  • Brush the lashes gently with a clean spoolie, from the middle to the tips.
  • Keep oil based products away from the eye area. This includes many cleansing balms and eye creams.

After that

Clean your lashes a few times a week with a lash safe cleanser and let them air dry or use a cool hair dryer setting. Never use a mechanical lash curler on extensions, and skip mascara. You will not need it.

When to call

A little redness right after the appointment can be normal. If you notice swelling, itching or irritation that does not settle down, contact Kelsey right away and talk to a medical professional if it gets worse.
